About

I am a Postdoctoral Associate in the Cyber Security and Privacy Lab at New York University Abu Dhabi, working with Dr. Christina Pöpper. My research investigates the reliability, security, and privacy of mobile and wireless systems, with a particular focus on positioning and localization in real-world network deployments.

I received my PhD in Telematics Engineering from Universidad Carlos III de Madrid, where I worked on positioning systems and the security and privacy challenges of real-world wireless network deployments. My broader research combines wireless measurements, protocol analysis, and machine learning to study vulnerabilities and design more robust mobile and cellular networks.

My research has appeared in venues including ACM MobiCom, ACM WiSec, and IEEE Transactions on Mobile Computing, and has contributed to an Ericsson patent on integrity monitoring for 5G Timing Advance.
