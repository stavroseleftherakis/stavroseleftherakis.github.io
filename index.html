Stavros Eleftherakis

Postdoctoral Associate, New York University Abu Dhabi
Cyber Security and Privacy Lab

I am a postdoctoral researcher at New York University Abu Dhabi. My research focuses on secure positioning systems, wireless security, privacy in cellular networks, and AI-driven analysis for wireless systems.

I received my PhD in Telematics Engineering from Universidad Carlos III de Madrid, where I worked on positioning systems and the security and privacy challenges of real-world wireless network deployments. My work spans WiFi and 5G positioning, privacy vulnerabilities in 5G standalone networks, and secure wireless infrastructure.

My research has appeared in venues including ACM MobiCom, ACM WiSec, and IEEE Transactions on Mobile Computing, and has contributed to an Ericsson patent on integrity monitoring for 5G Timing Advance.
